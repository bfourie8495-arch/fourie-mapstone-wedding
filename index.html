You’re invited

One wedding. Two surnames. Only one survives.

We’re getting married, and we can’t agree whose surname wins. So we’re letting you decide. Join us for a long weekend in the southern Drakensberg where Team Fourie and Team Mapstone battle it out across speeches, a pub quiz, field games and one very sneaky golden egg. The winning name is revealed at the ceremony on Sunday, when the officiant says “I now pronounce you Mr and Mrs…”.

The competition

How you win it for your side

General Election

Friday night after the speeches. Secret ballot for the best surname. You can vote for the other side, if they’ve earned it.

Pub Quiz

Six rounds, including how well you know the couple and each family’s trivia about the other.

Field Games

5 km run, touch rugby, tug of war and capture the flag. Everyone plays at least two events.

Party Games

Flip cup, beer pong and more. Every drinking game has a non-alcoholic option that scores the same.

The Golden Egg

Everyone gets a golden egg to wear all weekend. Protect yours, steal theirs, and bring them back to your team’s nest. No tackling.

Getting there

Khotso Lodge, Underberg

By road

  • From Durban: about 2.5–3 hours via the N3 and R617.
  • From Pietermaritzburg: about 2 hours.
  • From Johannesburg: about 6 hours.
  • Fill up in Underberg; the last stretch is on farm roads.

Flying in

Fly into King Shaka International (Durban) and hire a car or join the shared shuttle. Tell us in your RSVP if you’d use a shuttle and we’ll organise one if there’s enough interest.

Where to stay

Camp or cosy up

Camping

Most of us will be camping. Khotso’s campsite has hosted 150 people at once, right by the river. Bring your tent and the warmest sleeping bag you own: May nights in Underberg get close to freezing. Camping rates will be shared once confirmed with the lodge.

Beds

Khotso has about 50 beds: a log cabin for up to 20 and rondavels and lodge rooms for about 30. These will go fast, so tell us in your RSVP if you need a bed. There are also guesthouses in Underberg town, a short drive away.

What to pack

  • Warm layers, beanie and gloves for evenings
  • Trainers for game day, plus a change of clothes
  • Sunscreen and a hat (the mountain sun is strong)
  • Something nice for Saturday night and Sunday
  • Headlamp or torch

Dress code

  • Friday: casual and warm
  • Saturday day: sports kit in your team colours
  • Saturday night: smart casual, party ready
  • Sunday ceremony: Sunday best (layers welcome)

Questions

Good to know

Do I have to compete?

Not at all. There are cheering, refereeing and scorekeeping jobs too, and the Golden Egg is about sneakiness, not fitness.

Can I choose my team?

Family plays for their own surname. Everyone else gets drafted by the captains in February 2028. Bribes to the captains are welcome but not guaranteed to work.

Are kids welcome?

Yes. Kids get their own events that still score points for their team.

Can I bring a plus-one?

If your invitation names a plus-one, yes. Ask them to fill in their own RSVP so they get their own kit.

What about gifts?

Your company for the weekend is the gift. Registry details, if any, will be added here closer to the time.

What if the weather turns?

Every outdoor event has an indoor backup. Pack warm either way.
